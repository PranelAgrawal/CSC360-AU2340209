## 1. Linear Systems & Geometric Modeling: The Mathematics of a Triangle

To bound a closed polygonal region such as a triangle in a 2-dimensional Cartesian plane ($\mathbb{R}^2$), we rely on systems of linear algebraic equations and linear inequalities.

### The Linear System Representation: $Ax = b$

A linear equation representing an infinite line in $\mathbb{R}^2$ is written in standard form as:

$$a_i x + b_i y = c_i$$

When framing a system of three intersecting lines that define the boundaries of a triangle, we express it compactly using matrix-vector notation:

$$A\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{b}$$

Where:
* **Coefficient Matrix ($A \in \mathbb{R}^{3 \times 2}$):**
  $$A = \begin{bmatrix} a_1 & b_1 \\ a_2 & b_2 \\ a_3 & b_3 \end{bmatrix}$$
* **State Vector ($\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^{2 \times 1}$):** A column vector of unknown coordinates $\mathbf{x} = \begin{bmatrix} x \\ y \end{bmatrix} = [x, y]^T$.
* **Constant Vector ($\mathbf{b} \in \mathbb{R}^{3 \times 1}$):**
  $$\mathbf{b} = \begin{bmatrix} c_1 \\ c_2 \\ c_3 \end{bmatrix}$$

> **Note on Tooling:** Symbolic math libraries and build/automation environments (e.g., Maven-managed scientific Java libraries such as Apache Commons Math or EJML) allow developers to set up parameter matrices abstractly. This abstracts numerical methods (e.g., LU decomposition, QR factorization) away from manual arithmetic.

---

### Degrees of Freedom and Boundary Determinacy

* **Underdetermined State ($< 3$ Lines):** Two non-parallel lines intersect at exactly one point ($A \in \mathbb{R}^{2 \times 2}$). They divide the plane into four open, unbounded quadrants. Without a third constraining boundary, the closed triangular face cannot exist; there are infinitely many ways to complete the region.
* **Fully Determined Region ($3$ Boundary Lines):** Three distinct lines form a triangle if and only if:
    1. No two lines are parallel: $\frac{a_1}{b_1} \neq \frac{a_2}{b_2}$, $\frac{a_2}{b_2} \neq \frac{a_3}{b_3}$, $\frac{a_1}{b_1} \neq \frac{a_3}{b_3}$.
    2. The lines are non-concurrent (they do not all intersect at a single shared point, meaning $\det([A \mid \mathbf{b}]) \neq 0$).

---

### Coordinate Extraction: Pairwise Intersection Algorithm

To locate the three geometric vertices $V_1, V_2, V_3$, solve the three independent $2 \times 2$ sub-systems:

1. **Vertex 1 ($V_1 = L_1 \cap L_2$):** Solve $\begin{bmatrix} a_1 & b_1 \\ a_2 & b_2 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} x \\ y \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} c_1 \\ c_2 \end{bmatrix} \implies (x_1, y_1)$
2. **Vertex 2 ($V_2 = L_2 \cap L_3$):** Solve $\begin{bmatrix} a_2 & b_2 \\ a_3 & b_3 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} x \\ y \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} c_2 \\ c_3 \end{bmatrix} \implies (x_2, y_2)$
3. **Vertex 3 ($V_3 = L_3 \cap L_1$):** Solve $\begin{bmatrix} a_3 & b_3 \\ a_1 & b_1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} x \\ y \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} c_3 \\ c_1 \end{bmatrix} \implies (x_3, y_3)$

Connecting $V_1 \to V_2 \to V_3 \to V_1$ yields the polygonal perimeter. Replacing equality ($=$) with inequalities ($\le$ or $\ge$) defines the convex hull: the closed interior area of the triangle.

## 2. Linear Abstract Data Types: Stack vs. Queue

Stacks and Queues are sequential, constrained data structures governed by explicit rules for element insertion and removal.

| Property | Stack | Queue |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Operational Discipline** | **LIFO** (Last-In, First-Out) | **FIFO** (First-In, First-Out) |
| **Access Points** | Single-ended: insertion and deletion occur at the **Top**. | Double-ended: insertion at **Rear/Tail**, removal at **Front/Head**. |
| **Primary Operations** | `push(item)`, `pop()`, `peek()` | `enqueue(item)`, `dequeue()`, `peek()` |
| **Algorithmic Time Complexity** | $O(1)$ amortized for push/pop | $O(1)$ for enqueue/dequeue |
| **Canonical Use Case** | Backtracking, function call stacks, syntax parsing. | Buffering, resource scheduling, multi-threaded pipelines. |

---

### Concrete Execution Trace: The "Undo" (`Ctrl + Z`) Pattern

Consider a text editor tracking user actions:

* Action 1: Type `"Hello"`
* Action 2: Type `" World"`
* Action 3: Apply **Bold** styling

#### Why Queues Fail for `Ctrl + Z`:
A Queue respects arrival order (FIFO). If an undo engine queried a Queue, it would pop the *first* operation performed:
$$\text{Dequeue} \implies \text{Undo "Hello"}$$
This breaks user expectations: early baseline work is erased while leaving the most recent dependent operations orphaned.

#### Why Stacks Are Required:
A Stack stores historical snapshots by pushing each state onto the top of the runtime structure:
$$\text{Stack: } [\text{"Hello"}] \to [\text{"Hello"}, \text{" World"}] \to [\text{"Hello"}, \text{" World"}, \text{"Bold"} \ (\text{Top})]$$
Triggering `Ctrl + Z` calls `pop()`, targeting the latest operation:
$$\text{Pop} \implies \text{Undo "Bold"}$$
This preserves causal chronological coherence.
